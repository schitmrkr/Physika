### Simplifying the Einstein equations

Our Ricci tensor still contains several terms. We want to simplify it into something resembling a wave equation.

For this, we introduce the trace-reversed metric perturbation:

$$
\bar{h}_{\mu \nu} = h_{\mu \nu} - \frac{1}{2} \eta_{\mu \nu} h
$$

Because the Einstein tensor contains both the Ricci tensor and its trace:

$$
G_{\mu \nu} = R_{\mu \nu} - \frac{1}{2} g_{\mu \nu} R
$$

The trace-reversed perturbation combines these terms in a way that simplifies the equations.

In four dimensions, its trace is:

$$
\bar{h} = h - 2h = - h
$$

Therefore:

$$
h_{\mu \nu} = \bar{h}_{\mu \nu} - \frac{1}{2} \eta_{\mu \nu} \bar{h}
$$
