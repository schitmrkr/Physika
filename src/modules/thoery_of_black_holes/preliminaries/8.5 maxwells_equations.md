### Maxwell's equations

In the Newman-Penrose formalism, the antisymmetric Maxwell tensor, $F_{ij}$, is replaced by the three complex scalars.

$$
\phi_0 = F_{13} = F_{ij} l^i m^j,\\
\phi_1 = \frac{1}{2} F_{F_{12} + F_{43}} = \frac{1}{2} F_{ij}(l^i n^j + \bar{m}^i m^j), \\
\phi_2 = F_{42} = F_{ij} \bar{m}^i n^j
$$

and Maxwell's equations,

$$
F_{[ij;k]} = 0, \qquad g^{ik} F_{ij;k} = 0
$$

expressed in terms of  tetrad components and intrinsic derivatives, namely,

$$
F_{[ab|c]} = 0, \qquad \eta^{nm} F_{an|m} = 0
$$

are replaced by the equations,

$$
\phi_{1|1} - \phi_{0|4} = 0, \qquad \phi_{2|1} - \phi_{1|4} = 0, \\
\phi_{1|3} - \phi_{0|2} = 0, \qquad \phi_{2|3} - \phi_{1|2} = 0
$$

The explicit forms of these equations are readily found. Thus,

$$
\phi_{1|1} = \frac{1}{2} [F_{12,1} - \eta^{nm}(\gamma_{n11} F_{m2} + \gamma_{n21}F_{1m}) \\
+ F_{43,1} - \eta^{nm} (\gamma_{n41} F_{m3} + \gamma_{n31}F_{4m})] \\
= \phi_{1,1} - (\gamma_{131}F_{42} + \gamma_{241}F_{13}) \\
= D \phi_1 + \kappa \phi_2 - \pi \phi_0
$$

and similarly,

$$
\phi_{0|4} = \delta^* \phi_0 - 2 \alpha \phi_0 + 2 \rho \phi_1
$$

The first of Maxwell's equations, therefore, take the form

$$
D \phi_1 - \delta^* \phi_0 = (\pi - 2 \alpha) \phi_0 + 2 \rho \phi_1 - \kappa \phi_2
$$

And the remaining equations are

$$
D \phi_2 - \delta^* \phi_1 = - \lambda \phi_0 + 2 \pi \phi_1 + (\rho - 2 \epsilon) \phi_2
$$

$$
\delta \phi_1 - \Delta \phi_0 = (\mu - 2\lambda) \phi_0 + 2 \tau \phi_1 - \sigma \phi_2
$$

$$
\delta \phi_2 - \Delta \phi_1 = - \nu \phi_0 + 2 \mu \phi_1 + (\tau - 2 \beta) \phi_2
$$

Turning next to the energy-momentum tensor of the Maxwell field, we have

$$
T_{ab} = \eta^{cd} F_{ac} F_{bd} - \frac{1}{4} \eta_{ab} F_{ef} F^{ef}
$$

and in terms of the Maxwell scalars $\phi_0$, $\phi_1$, and $\phi_2$, we readily find that

$$
- \frac{1}{2} T_{11} = \phi_0 \phi_0^*; \qquad -\frac{1}{2} T_{13} = \phi_0 \phi_1^*;
$$

$$
- \frac{1}{4} (T_{13} + T_{34}) = \phi_1 \phi_1^*; \qquad -\frac{1}{2} T_{23} = \phi_1 \phi_2^*;
$$

$$
- \frac{1}{2} T_{22} = \phi_2 \phi_2^*; \qquad -\frac{1}{2} T_{33} = \phi_0 \phi_2^*;
$$

and the trace of $T_{ab}$ is, of course, zero. In accordance with equations 

$$
\Phi_{00} = \frac{1}{2} R_{11}; \space \Phi_{22} = - \frac{1}{2} R_{22}; \space \Phi_{02} = - \frac{1}{2} R_{33}; \space \Phi_{20} = - \frac{1}{2} R_{44}; \\
$$

$$
\Phi_{11} = - \frac{1}{4}(R_{12} + R_{34}); \space \Phi_{01} = - \frac{1}{2}R_{13}; \space \Phi_{10} = - \frac{1}{2}R_{14};
$$

$$
\Alpha = \frac{1}{24} R = \frac{1}{12}(R_{12} - R_{34}); \space \Phi_{12} = - \frac{1}{2} R_{23}; \space \Phi_{21} = - \frac{1}{2} R_{24}
$$

and

$$
R_{ij} = - \frac{8 \pi G}{c^4} (T_{ij} - \frac{1}{2} g_{ij} T)
$$

we can, therefore

$$
\text{set } \Lambda = 0 \text{ and replace } \Phi_{mn} \text{ by } \phi_m \phi_n^*
$$

apart from the constant proportionality, $-8 \pi G / c^4$.

In the Bianchi identities listed in equations before this from $(a-h)$ with Weyl and Ricci terms simplify considerably when use is made of Maxwell's equation. Thus, considering the terms in the derivatives of the Ricci scalars in equation $a$ of the Bianchi identity, we have

$$
- D \Phi_{01} + \delta \Phi_{00} \to - D (\phi_0 \phi_1^*) + \delta (\phi_0 \phi_0^*)  \\
= - \phi_0 (D \phi_1^* - \delta \phi_0^*) - \phi_1^* D \phi_0 + \phi_0^* \delta \phi_0 \\
= - \phi_0 \{ (\pi^* - 2 \alpha^*) \phi_0^* + 2 \rho^* \phi_1^* - \kappa^* \phi_2^* \}\\
- \phi_1^* D \phi_0 + \phi_0^* \delta \phi_0
$$

and inserting this last expression in equation $a$ of the Ricci scalar in Bianchi identity, we find that we are left with

$$
- \phi_1^* D \phi_0 + \phi_0^* \delta \phi_0 + 2 (\epsilon \phi_0 \phi_1^* + \sigma \phi_1 \phi_0^* - \kappa \phi_1 \phi_1^* - \beta \phi_0 \phi_0^* )
$$

The other terms listed allow of similar simplifications; and the Ricci terms to be included in equations of Bianchi identities for an Einstein-Maxwell field take the following forms(aprat from a constant of proportionality):

$$
-\phi_1^*D\phi_0+\phi_0^*\delta\phi_0+2(\epsilon\phi_0\phi_1^*+\sigma\phi_1\phi_0^*-\kappa\phi_1\phi_1^*-\beta\phi_0\phi_0^*)
$$

$$
+\phi_1^*\delta^*\phi_0-\phi_0^*\Delta\phi_0+2(-\alpha\phi_0\phi_1^*+\rho\phi_1\phi_1^*+\gamma\phi_0\phi_1^*-\mu\phi_0\phi_0^*)
$$

$$
-\phi_1^*D\phi_2+\phi_0^*\delta\phi_2+2(-\epsilon\phi_2\phi_1^*-\lambda\phi_1\phi_0^*+\beta\phi_2\phi_0^*+\pi\phi_1\phi_1^*)
$$

$$
-\phi_0^*\Delta\phi_2+\phi_1^*\delta^*\phi_2+2(\alpha\phi_2\phi_1^*+\nu\phi_1\phi_0^*-\gamma\phi_2\phi_0^*-\tau\phi_1\phi_1^*)
$$

$$
-\phi_2^*D\phi_0+\phi_1^*\delta\phi_0+2(-\kappa\phi_1\phi_2^*-\beta\phi_0\phi_2^*+\pi\phi_1\phi_1^*+\epsilon\phi_0\phi_2^*)
$$

$$
+\phi_1^*\Delta\phi_0-\phi_2^*\delta^*\phi_0+2(-\rho\phi_1\phi_2^*-\gamma\phi_0\phi_2^*+\tau\phi_1\phi_1^*+\alpha\phi_0\phi_2^*)
$$

$$
-\phi_2^*D\phi_2+\phi_1^*\delta\phi_2+2(-\epsilon\phi_2\phi_2^*-\mu\phi_1\phi_2^*+\beta\phi_2\phi_1^*+\pi\phi_1\phi_2^*)
$$

$$
+\phi_1^*\Delta\phi_2-\phi_2^*\delta^*\phi_2+2(-\alpha\phi_2\phi_2^*+\nu\phi_1\phi_2^*-\gamma\phi_2\phi_1^*-\tau\phi_1\phi_2^*)
$$
