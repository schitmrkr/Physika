### The cyclic and the Bianchi identities in case the torsion is zero

In case the torsion is zero

$$
\nabla_X Q - \nabla_Q X = [X, Q], \space (X, Q \in T^1_0)
$$

First we verify that,

$$
\nabla_X [Y,Z] + \nabla_Y [Z,X] + \nabla_Z [X,Y] \\
= (\nabla_X \nabla_Y - \nabla_Y \nabla_X) Z + (\nabla_Z \nabla_X - \nabla_X \nabla_Z) Y  + (\nabla_Y \nabla_Z - \nabla_Z \nabla_Y) X 
$$

Therefore,

$$
R(X,Y)Z + R(Z,X)Y + R(Y,Z)X = \nabla_X [Y,Z] + \nabla_Y [Z,X] + \nabla_Z [X,Y] - \nabla_{[X,Y]} Z - \nabla_{[Y,Z]} X - \nabla_{[Z,X]} Y
$$

On the otherhand by writing $Q=[Y,Z]$, we obtain,

$$
\nabla_X [Y,Z] - \nabla_{[Y,Z]}X = [X, [Y,Z]] 
$$

Thus the curvature equation reduces to 

$$
R(X,Y)Z + R(Z,X)Y + R(Y,Z)X = [X, [Y,Z]] + [Y, [Z,X]] + [Z, [X,Y]] = 0 
$$

by the Jacobi identity. In a local coordinate basis, the above equation provides the cyclic identity

$$
R^j_{lkm} + R^j_{klm} + R^j_{mlk} = 0
$$

Next consider the exterior derivative of Cartan's two form $\Omega^j_l$. We have

$$
d \Omega^j_l = d\omega^j_k \wedge \omega^k_l - \omega^j_k \wedge d\omega^k_l \\
= (\Omega^j_k - \omega^j_m \wedge \omega^m_k) \wedge \omega^k_l - \omega^j_k \wedge (\Omega^k_l - \omega^k_m \wedge \omega^m_l)
$$

the triple wedge products of the oneforms which occur cancel each other,

$$
d\Omega^j_l - \Omega^j_k \wedge \omega^k_l + \omega^j_k \wedge \Omega^k_l = 0
$$

This equation expresses Bianchi identities.
