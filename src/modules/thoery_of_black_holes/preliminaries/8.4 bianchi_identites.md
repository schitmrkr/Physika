### The Bianchi identities

As we have stated earlier , there are, altogether, twenty linearly-independent Bianchi identities. A complete set is provided by the eight complex identities

$$
R_{13[13|4]} = 0; \space R_{13[21|4]} = 0; \space R_{13[13|2]} = 0; \space R_{13[43|2]} = 0; \space \\
R_{42[13|4]} = 0; \space R_{42[21|4]} = 0; \space R_{42[13|2]} = 0; \space R_{42[43|2]} = 0; 
$$

and the four real identites which follow from

$$
\eta^{bc} (R_{ab} - \frac{1}{2}\eta_{ab}R)_{|c} = 0
$$

Explicitly written out, the above equation provides the two real equations.

$$
R_{11|2} + R_{34|1} - R_{13|4} - R_{14|3} = 0 \\
R_{22|1} + R_{34|2} - R_{23|4} - R_{24|3} = 0 
$$

and one complex equation

$$
R_{33|4} + R_{12|3} - R_{31|2} - R_{32|1} = 0 
$$

We shall write down the explict forms of the various identities in terms of the spin coefficients and the Weyl and the Ricci scalars. As an example consider the first of the identities listed

$$
R_{1313|4} + R_{1334|1} + R_{1341|3} = 0
$$

By making use of relations of Riemann with Weyl, Ricci

$$
C_{1313|4} + (C_{1334} + \frac{1}{2} R_{13})_{|1} - \frac{1}{2} R_{11|3} = 0
$$

Considering the terms in the Weyl tensor, we have

$$
C_{1313|4} = C_{1313,4} - \eta^{nm} (\gamma_{n14} C_{m313} + \gamma_{n34}C_{1m13} \\
+ \gamma_{n14} C_{13m3} + \gamma_{n34} C_{131m}) \\
= C_{1313,4} - 2(\gamma_{214} + \gamma_{344})C_{1313} + 2 \gamma_{314} (C_{1213} + C_{4313}) \\
 = - \delta^* \Psi_0 + 4 \alpha \Psi_0 - 4 \rho \Psi_1
$$

and 

$$
C_{1334|1} = C_{1334,1} + \eta^{nm} [\gamma_{n11} C_{m334} + \gamma_{n31} (C_{1m34} + C_{13m4}) \\
+ \gamma_{n41} C_{133m}] \\
= C_{1334,1} + [(\gamma_{211} + \gamma_{341})C_{1334} + \gamma_{131}(C_{1234} - C_{3434}) \\
+ \gamma_{231} C_{1314} + \gamma_{141} C_{1332} + \gamma_{131} C_{1324} + \gamma_{241} C_{1331}] \\
= D \Psi_1 - 2 \epsilon \Psi_1 + 3 \kappa \Psi_2 - \pi \Psi_0
$$

Similarly, we find

$$
\frac{1}{2}(R_{13|1} - R_{11|3}) = - D \Phi_{01} + \delta \Phi_{00} + 2(\epsilon + \rho^*) \Phi_{01} + 2 \sigma \Phi_{10}\\
- 2 \kappa \Phi_{11} - \kappa^* \Phi_{02} + (\pi^* - 2 \alpha^* - 2 \beta) \Phi_{00}
$$

The remaining identites can be derived in similar fashion.

### The eight complex Bianchi identities

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{(a)}\qquad
-\delta^*\Psi_0
+D\Psi_1
+(4\alpha-\pi)\Psi_0
-2(2\rho+\epsilon)\Psi_1
+3\kappa\Psi_2
+[{\rm Ricci}]_a
=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{(b)}\qquad
-\Delta\Psi_1
+\delta\Psi_2
+\nu\Psi_0
+2(\gamma-\mu)\Psi_1
-3\tau\Psi_2
+2\sigma\Psi_3
+[{\rm Ricci}]_b
=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{(c)}\qquad
-\delta^*\Psi_2
+D\Psi_3
+2\lambda\Psi_1
-3\pi\Psi_2
+2(\epsilon-\rho)\Psi_3
+\kappa\Psi_4
+[{\rm Ricci}]_c
=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{(d)}\qquad
\delta^*\Psi_3
-D\Psi_4
-3\lambda\Psi_2
+2(2\pi+\alpha)\Psi_3
-(4\epsilon-\rho)\Psi_4
+[{\rm Ricci}]_d
=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{(e)}\qquad
-\Delta\Psi_0
+\delta\Psi_1
+(4\gamma-\mu)\Psi_0
-2(2\tau+\beta)\Psi_1
+3\sigma\Psi_2
+[{\rm Ricci}]_e
=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{(f)}\qquad
-\Delta\Psi_1
+\delta\Psi_2
+\nu\Psi_0
+2(\gamma-\mu)\Psi_1
-3\tau\Psi_2
+2\sigma\Psi_3
+[{\rm Ricci}]_f
=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{(g)}\qquad
-\Delta\Psi_2
+\delta\Psi_3
+2\nu\Psi_1
-3\mu\Psi_2
+2(\beta-\tau)\Psi_3
+\sigma\Psi_4
+[{\rm Ricci}]_g
=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{(h)}\qquad
-\Delta\Psi_3
+\delta\Psi_4
+3\nu\Psi_2
-2(\gamma+2\mu)\Psi_3
-(\tau-4\beta)\Psi_4
+[{\rm Ricci}]_h
=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

### Ricci terms in the Bianchi identities

$$
\begin{aligned}
[{\rm Ricci}]_a
={}&
-D\Phi_{01}
+\delta\Phi_{00}
+2(\epsilon+\rho^*)\Phi_{01}
+2\sigma\Phi_{10}
\\
&-2\kappa\Phi_{11}
-\kappa^*\Phi_{02}
+(\pi^*-2\alpha^*-2\beta)\Phi_{00}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
[{\rm Ricci}]_b
={}&
\delta^*\Phi_{01}
-\Delta\Phi_{00}
-2(\alpha+\tau^*)\Phi_{01}
+2\rho\Phi_{11}
+\sigma^*\Phi_{02}
\\
&-(\mu^*-2\gamma-2\gamma^*)\Phi_{00}
-2\tau\Phi_{10}
-2D\Lambda.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
[{\rm Ricci}]_c
={}&
-D\Phi_{21}
+\delta\Phi_{20}
+2(\rho^*-\epsilon)\Phi_{21}
-2\mu\Phi_{10}
+2\pi\Phi_{11}
\\
&-\kappa^*\Phi_{22}
-(2\alpha^*-2\beta-\pi^*)\Phi_{20}
-2\delta^*\Lambda.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
[{\rm Ricci}]_d
={}&
-\Delta\Phi_{20}
+\delta^*\Phi_{21}
+2(\alpha-\tau^*)\Phi_{21}
+2\nu\Phi_{10}
+\sigma^*\Phi_{22}
\\
&-2\lambda\Phi_{11}
-(\mu^*+2\gamma-2\gamma^*)\Phi_{20}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
[{\rm Ricci}]_e
={}&
-D\Phi_{02}
+\delta\Phi_{01}
+2(\pi^*-\beta)\Phi_{01}
-2\kappa\Phi_{12}
-\lambda^*\Phi_{00}
\\
&+2\sigma\Phi_{11}
+(\rho^*+2\epsilon-2\epsilon^*)\Phi_{02}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
[{\rm Ricci}]_f
={}&
\Delta\Phi_{01}
-\delta^*\Phi_{02}
+2(\mu^*-\gamma)\Phi_{01}
-2\rho\Phi_{12}
-\nu^*\Phi_{00}
\\
&+2\tau\Phi_{11}
+(\tau^*-2\beta^*+2\alpha)\Phi_{02}
+2\delta\Lambda.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
[{\rm Ricci}]_g
={}&
-D\Phi_{22}
+\delta\Phi_{21}
+2(\pi^*+\beta)\Phi_{21}
-2\mu\Phi_{11}
-\lambda^*\Phi_{20}
\\
&+2\pi\Phi_{12}
+(\rho^*-2\epsilon-2\epsilon^*)\Phi_{22}
-2\Delta\Lambda.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
[{\rm Ricci}]_h
={}&
\Delta\Phi_{21}
-\delta^*\Phi_{22}
+2(\mu^*+\gamma)\Phi_{21}
-2\nu\Phi_{11}
-\nu^*\Phi_{20}
\\
&+2\lambda\Phi_{12}
+(\tau^*-2\alpha-2\beta^*)\Phi_{22}.
\end{aligned}
$$
