### The commutation relations and the structure constants

The Lie bracket, $[e_{(a)}, e_{(b)}]$ plays an important role in the theory; and being a tangent vector itself, can be expanded in terms of the same basis, $e_{(a)}$. Thus, we must have an expansion of the form

$$
[e_{(a)}, e_{(b)}] = C^{(c)}_{(a)(b)} e_{(c)}
$$

The coefficient $ C^{(c)}_{(a)(b)}$ in this expansion is called the structure constants; they are antisymmetric in the indices $(a)$ and $(b)$.

The structure constants can be expressed in terms of the rotation coefficients as follows. Consider the effect of Lie bracket on scalar field $f$.

$$
[e_{(a)}, e_{(b)}]f = e^i_{(a)} [e^j_{(b)} f_{,j}]_{,i} - e^i_{(b)} [e^j_{(a)} f_{,j}]_{,i} \\ 
= [e^i_{(a)} e^j_{(b);i} - e^i_{(b)} e^j_{(a);i} ] f_{,j} \\
= [-\gamma^j_{(b)(a)} + \gamma^j_{(a)(b)}] f_{,j} \\
= [- \gamma^{(c)}_{(b)(a)} + \gamma^{(c)}_{(a)(b)}] e^j_{(c)} f_{,j}
$$

Therefore,

$$
C^{(c)}_{(a)(b)} = \gamma^{(c)}_{(b)(a)} - \gamma^{(c)}_{(a)(b)}
$$

***

**Notes**

$$
[e_{(a)}, e_{(b)}]f = e^i_{(a)} \partial_i [e^j_{(b)} f_{,j}] - e^i_{(b)} \partial_i [e^j_{(a)} f_{,j}]
$$

Now,

$$
e^i_{(a)} \partial_i [e^j_{(b)} f_{,j}] = e^i_{(a)} (\partial_i e^j_{(b)}) f_{,j} + e^i_{(b)} e^j_{(a)} \partial_i \partial_j f
$$

Similarly,

$$
e^i_{(b)} \partial_i [e^j_{(a)} f_{,j}] = e^i_{(b)} (\partial_i e^j_{(a)}) f_{,j} + e^i_{(b)} e^j_{(a)} \partial_i \partial_j f
$$

So,

$$
[e_{(a)}, e_{(b)}]f = [e^i_{(a)} (\partial_i e^j_{(b)}) - e^i_{(b)} (\partial_i e^j_{(a)})] f_{,j} + [e^i_{(b)} e^j_{(a)} - e^i_{(b)} e^j_{(a)}] \partial_i \partial_j f
$$

We know,

$$
[e^i_{(b)} e^j_{(a)} - e^i_{(b)} e^j_{(a)}] \partial_i \partial_j f = 0
$$

So,

$$
[e_{(a)}, e_{(b)}]f = [e^i_{(a)} (\partial_i e^j_{(b)}) - e^i_{(b)} (\partial_i e^j_{(a)})] f_{,j}
$$

Since the Christoffel contribution dissapears, we can write

$$
[e_{(a)}, e_{(b)}]f = [e^i_{(a)} e^j_{(b);j} - e^i_{(b)} e^j_{(a);i}] f_{,j}
$$
