### The commutation relations and the structure constants

We consider first the commutation relation 

$$
[e_{(a)},e_{(b)}] = (\gamma_{cba} - \gamma_{cab}) e^c = C^c_{ab} e_c
$$

As an example, consider

$$
[\Delta, D] = [n, l] = [e_2, e_1]= (\gamma_{c12} - \gamma_{c21}) e^c \\
= - \gamma_{121} e^1 + \gamma_{212} e^2 + (\gamma_{312} - \gamma_{321}) e^3 + \gamma_{412} - \gamma_{421}) e^4 \\
= - \gamma_{121} \Delta + \gamma_{212} D + (\gamma_{312} - \gamma_{321}) \delta^* + \gamma_{412} - \gamma_{421}) \delta
$$

or, giving the spin coefficients their designated symbols, we obtain

$$
\Delta D - D \Delta = (\gamma + \gamma^*)D + (\epsilon + \epsilon^*)\Delta - (\tau^* + \pi)\delta - (\tau + \pi^*)\delta^*
$$

In similar fashion, we obtain

$$
\delta D - D \delta = (\alpha^* + \beta - \pi^*)D + \kappa \Delta - (\rho^* + \epsilon - \epsilon^*) \delta + \sigma \delta^*
$$

$$
\delta \Delta - \Delta \delta = - \nu^* D + (\tau - \alpha^* - \beta) \Delta + (\mu - \gamma + \gamma^*) \delta + \lambda^* \delta^*
$$

$$
\delta^* \delta - \delta \delta^* = (\mu^* - \mu)D + (\rho^* - \rho) \Delta + (\alpha - \beta^*) \delta + (\beta - \alpha^*) \delta^*
$$

By expressing the foregoing relations in the manner of commuatation equation, we find that the structure constants are related to the spin coefficients as in the accompanying tabulation:

$$
C^1_{21} = (\gamma + \gamma^*); \space C^2_{21} =  (\epsilon + \epsilon^*); \space C^2_{21} = - (\tau^* + \pi); \space C^3_{21} = - (\tau +\pi^*);
$$

$$
C^1_{31} = (\alpha^* + \beta - \pi^*); \space C^2_{31} =  \kappa; \space C^2_{31} = - (\rho^* + \epsilon - \epsilon^*); \space C^3_{31} = \sigma;
$$

$$
C^1_{32} = - \nu^*; \space C^2_{32} =  (\tau - \alpha^* - \beta); \space C^2_{32} = (\mu - \gamma + \gamma^*); \space C^3_{32} = \lambda^*;
$$

$$
C^1_{43} = (\mu^* - \mu); \space C^2_{43} =  (\rho^* - \rho); \space C^2_{43} = (\alpha - \beta^*); \space C^3_{43} = (\beta - \alpha^*);
$$
