### Riemann Tensor

$$
R(X,Y) = \nabla_X \nabla_Y - \nabla_Y \nabla_X - \nabla_{[X,Y]}
$$

Lets analyse the right hand side of equation inn local coordinate basis

$$
\nabla_X \nabla_Y Z = \nabla_X (Y^k Z^j_{;k} e_j) = Y^k \nabla_k (Z^j_{;k} e_j) + Z^j_{;k} e_j \nabla_X Y^k
$$

$$
= Y^k X^l Z^j_{;k;l} e_j + Y^k_{;l} X^l Z^j_{;k} e_j
$$

Accordingly,

$$
\nabla_X \nabla_Y Z - \nabla_Y \nabla_X Z = Y^k X^l (Z^j_{;k;l} - Z^j_{;l;k}) e_j + [Y^k_{;l} X^l - X^k_{;l} Y^l] Z^j_{;k} e_j
$$

$$
= Y^k X^l (Z^j_{;k;l} - Z^j_{;l;k}) e_j  + [X, Y]^k Z^j_{;k} e_j + X^l Y^n(\Gamma^k_{nl} - \Gamma^k_{ln}) Z^j_{;k} e_j
$$

Since,

$$
\nabla_{[X, Y]}Z = [X, Y]^k Z^j_{;k} e_j
$$

$$
R(X, Y)Z = (Z^j_{;k;l} - Z^j_{;l;k} + T^n_{kl}Z^j_{;n}) X^l Y^k e_j 
$$

$$
= R^j_{ilk} Z^i X^l Y^k e_j
$$

Thus we obtain the relation,

$$
Z^j_{;k;l} - Z^j_{;l;k} = - R^j_{ikl} Z^i + T^n_{kl} Z^j_{;n} 
$$

This is the Ricci identity.

**Notes**

Notation:

$$
Z^j_{;k} = \nabla_k Z^j 
$$

$$
\nabla_Y Z = Y^k \nabla_k Z^j 
$$

$$
\nabla_Y Z = Y^k Z^j_{;k} e_j
$$

In equation,

$$
\nabla_X \nabla_Y Z - \nabla_Y \nabla_X Z = Y^k X^l (Z^j_{;k;l} - Z^j_{;l;k}) e_j + [Y^k_{;l} X^l - X^k_{;l} Y^l] Z^j_{;k} e_j
$$

We have $[Y^k_{;l} X^l - X^k_{;l} Y^l] Z^j_{;k} e_j$ which at first looks unrelated to Lie bracket. But it is almost Lie bracket. Recall:

$$
[X, Y]^k = X^l \partial_l Y^k - Y^l \partial_l X^k
$$

But covariant derivative contains Christofell symbol

$$
Y^k_{;l} = \partial_l Y^k + \Gamma^k_{nl} Y^n
$$

$$
X^k_{;l} = \partial_l X^k + \Gamma^k_{nl} X^n
$$

Thus,

$$
Y^k_{;l} X^l - X^k_{;l} Y^l = [X, Y]^k + X^l Y^n(\Gamma^k_{nl} - \Gamma^k_{ln})
$$

Recall torsion in coordinate expression,

$$
T^k_{ln} = \Gamma^k_{nl} - \Gamma^k_{ln}
$$

Therefore,

$$
Y^k_{;l} X^l - X^k_{;l} Y^l = [X, Y]^k + T^k_{nl} X^l Y^n
$$

It is of interest to contrast Ricci identity with the equation we previously derived.

$$
f_{,k;l} - f_{,l;k} = T^n_{kl} f_{,n}, \space T^n_{kl} = (\Gamma^n_{lk} - \Gamma^n_{kl})
$$

A further result of some improtance
$$
R(X, Y)f = \nabla_X(Y^j f_{,j}) - \nabla_Y{X^j f_{,j}} - [X, Y]^j f_{,j}
$$

$$
= (X^i Y^j_{;l} - Y^l X^j_{;l}) f_{,j} + Y^j X^l (f_{,j;l} - f_{,l;j}) - [X, Y]^j f_,j
$$

$$
= X^l Y^n (\Gamma^j_{nl} - \Gamma^j_{ln}) f_{,j} + X^l Y^j(- \Gamma^n_{jl} + \Gamma^n_{lj})f_{,n} = 0
$$

Now we shall consider the effect of $R(X, Y)$ on arbitrary tensor fields. By virtue of the Leibnitz rule followed by covariant differentiation of tensor products, we readily verify that

$$
R(X, Y)(P \otimes Q) = R(X, Y)P \otimes Q + P \otimes  R(X, Y) Q
$$

where $P$ and $Q$ are arbitrary tensor fields. We can find the effect of $R(X,Y)$ on a one-from $\Omega$. Thus if $Z$ is any contravariant vector-field, it follows

$$
R(X, Y)(\Omega_j Z^j) = [R(X, Y)\Omega]_j Z^j + \Omega_j [R(X, Y)Z]^j
$$

Since, $R(X,Y)$ acting on a scalar field vanishes, we conclude

$$
[R(X,Y) \Omega]_j Z^j = - \Omega_j R^j_{ilk} Z^i X^l Y^k = - \Omega_i R^i_{jlk} Z^j X^l Y^k
$$

Hence,

$$
[R(X,Y) \Omega]_j = - \Omega_i R^i_{jlk} X^l Y^k
$$

On other hand evaluating the effect of $R(X,Y)$ on $\Omega$ by the same procedure that was followed in deriving equation, we obtain

$$
R(X,Y) \Omega = X^l Y^k [\Omega_{j;k;l} - \Omega_{j;l;k} + T^n_{lk} \Omega_{j;n}] e^j
$$

Comparing the above two equations

$$
\Omega_{j;k;l} - \Omega_{j;l;k} = R^i_{jlk} \Omega_i + T^n_{kl} \Omega_{j;n}
$$

Again, considering the effect of $R(X,Y)$ on a tensor of type $(2,0)$, we have

$$
R(X, Y)[S^{ij} e_i \otimes e_j] = R(X, Y)S^{ij} e_i \otimes e_j + e_i \otimes R(X, Y) S^{ij} e_j \\
=(R^i_{lmn} S^{lj} + R^j_{lmn} S^{il}) X^m Y^n e_i \otimes e_j
$$

and we conclude that

$$
S^{ij}_{;k;l} - S^{ij}_{;l;k} = - R^i_{mkl} S^{mj} - R^j_{mkl} S^{mj} + T^n_{kl} S^{ij}_{;n}
$$

In similar fashion, we find

$$
S_{ij;k;l} - S^{ij;l;k} = R^m_{jkl} S_{im} + R^m_{ikl} S_{mj} + T^n_{kl} S_{ij;n}
$$


We note that by contracting the Riemann tensor with respect to the second(or third) covariant index, we obtain the Ricci tensor(or its negative); thus

$$
R^j_{ljm} = - R^j_{lmj} =  R_{lm}
$$

In local coordinate basis,

$$
R_{lm} = \Gamma^j_{lm,j} - \Gamma^j_{lj,m} + \Gamma^j_{kj} \Gamma^k_{lm} - \Gamma^j_{km} \Gamma^k_{ij}
$$
