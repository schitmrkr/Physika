### Some consequences of the Christoffel connection for the Riemann and the Ricci tensor

When the connection is that compatible with a metric, the Riemann and the Ricci tensors have additional symmetries. Thus, the equation

$$
S_{ij;k;l} - S_{ij;l;k} = R^m_{jkl} S_{im} + R^m_{ikl} S_{mj} + T^n_{kl} S_{ij;n}
$$

with $g_{ij}$ substituted for $S_{ij}$, gives

$$
g_{im}R^m_{jkl} + g_{mj}R^m_{ikl} = 0
$$

or, with the index $m$ lowered, we have

$$
R_{ijkl} + R_{jikl} = 0
$$

Hence, the completely covariant Riemann-tensor is antisymmetric in the first pair of indices as well. We deduce a further symmetry by the following sequence of transformations. Starting from cyclic identity

$$
R_{jkmn} + R_{jmnk} + R_{jnkm} = 0
$$

Making use of the known antisymmetry inthe first and second pair of indices and of the cyclic identity as well, we find 

$$
R_{jkmn} = - (R_{jmnk} + R_{jnkm}) = R_{mjnk} + R_{njkm} \\
= -(R_{mnjk} + R_{mkjn}) - (R_{nkmj} + R_{nmjk}) \\
= 2 R_{mnjk} + (R_{kmjn} + R_{knmj}) \\ 
= 2 R_{mnjk} - R_{kjnm} =  2 R_{mnjk} - R_{jkmn}
$$

Hence,

$$
R_{jkmn} = R_{mnjk}
$$

The Riemann tensor is therefore unchanged by the simultaneours interchanging of the first pair with the second pair of its covariant indices. From these symmetries, we conclude that the number of independent components of the Riemann tensor is $n^2(n^2-1)/12$ i.e. $20$ for the Riemann tensor fof a four dimensional manifold.

From symmetries of the Riemann tensor, the symmetry of teh Ricci tensor follows

$$
R_{ij} = g^{kl} R_{ikjl} = g^{lk} R_{ljki} = R_{ji}
$$

We may note in this connection the explicit expression for the Ricci tensor in local coordinate basis. But we first observe that

$$
\Gamma^j_{jk} = \frac{1}{2} g^{jl} (g_{ij,k} + g_{lk,j} - g_{jk,l}) = \frac{1}{2} g^{jl} g_{lj,k} \\ 
= (lg \sqrt{|g|})_{,k}
$$

where $|g|$ denotes the absolute value of the determinant of $[g_{ij}]$. With the foregoing expression for the contracted Christoffel symbol, equation 

gives

$$
R_{lm} = \Gamma^j_{lm,j} - \frac{\partial^2 lg \sqrt{|g|}}{\partial x^l \partial x^m} + \frac{\partial lg \sqrt{|g|}}{\partial x^k} \Gamma^k_{lm} - \Gamma^j_{km} \Gamma^k_{lj}
$$

Proof:

Let

$$
S = S_{ij} e^i \otimes e^j
$$

be covariant rank-2 tensor (2,0). 

Its covariant derivative is 

$$
S_{ij;k} = \partial_k S_{ij} - \Gamma^m_{ik} S_{mj} - \Gamma^m_{jk} S_{im}
$$

$\partial_k S_{ij}$ - ordinary change

$\Gamma^m_{ik} S_{mj}$ - connection acting on first index

$\Gamma^m_{jk} S_{im}$ - connection acting on second index

So,

$$
S_{ij;k;l} = \nabla_l (\nabla_k S)_{ij}
$$

$$
S_{ij;k;l} = \partial_l S_{ij;k} - \Gamma^m_{il} S_{mj;k} - \Gamma^m_{jl} S_{im;k} - \Gamma^m_{il} S_{mj;k} - \Gamma^m_{kl} S_{ij;m}
$$

Similarly,

$$
S_{ij;l;k} = \partial_k S_{ij;l} - \Gamma^m_{ik} S_{mj;l} - \Gamma^m_{jk} S_{im;l} - \Gamma^m_{lk} S_{ij;m}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij;k;l} - S_{ij;l;k}
={}&
\partial_l S_{ij;k}
-\partial_k S_{ij;l}
\\
&- \Gamma^m_{il} S_{mj;k}
+ \Gamma^m_{ik} S_{mj;l}
\\
&- \Gamma^m_{jl} S_{im;k}
+ \Gamma^m_{jk} S_{im;l}
\\
&- \Gamma^m_{kl} S_{ij;m}
+ \Gamma^m_{lk} S_{ij;m}
\end{aligned}
$$

Consider again,

$$
S_{ij;k} = \partial_k S_{ij} - \Gamma^m_{ik} S_{mj} - \Gamma^m_{jk} S_{im}
$$

$$
\partial_l S_{ij;k} = \partial_l (\partial_k S_{ij} - \Gamma^m_{ik} S_{mj} - \Gamma^m_{jk} S_{im})
$$

$$
\partial_l S_{ij;k} = \partial_l \partial_k S_{ij} - (\partial_l \Gamma^m_{ik}) S_{mj} - \Gamma^m_{ik} (\partial_l S_{mj})\\
 - (\partial_l \Gamma^m_{jk}) S_{im} - \Gamma^m_{jk} (\partial_l S_{im})
$$

$$
\partial_k S_{ij;l} = \partial_k \partial_l S_{ij} - (\partial_k \Gamma^m_{il}) S_{mj} - \Gamma^m_{il} (\partial_k S_{mj})\\
 - (\partial_k \Gamma^m_{jl}) S_{im} - \Gamma^m_{jl} (\partial_k S_{im})
$$

Therefore,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\partial_l S_{ij;k} - \partial_k S_{ij;l} 
={}& 
\partial_l \partial_k S_{ij} - \partial_k \partial_l S_{ij}
\\
&
- (\partial_l \Gamma^m_{ik}) S_{mj} + (\partial_k \Gamma^m_{il}) S_{mj}
\\
&
- \Gamma^m_{ik} (\partial_l S_{mj}) + \Gamma^m_{il} (\partial_k S_{mj})
\\
&
- (\partial_l \Gamma^m_{jk}) S_{im} + (\partial_k \Gamma^m_{jl}) S_{im}
\\
&
- \Gamma^m_{jk} (\partial_l S_{im}) + \Gamma^m_{jl} (\partial_k S_{im})
\end{aligned} 
$$

Since ordinary partial derivatives commute,

$$
\partial_l \partial_k S_{ij} = \partial_k \partial_l S_{ij}
$$

So, they vanish.

Expand terms involving $\Gamma^m_{il} S_{mj;k}$
