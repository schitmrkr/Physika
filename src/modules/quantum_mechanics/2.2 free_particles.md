### Free Particles


For a free particle, potential must be zero everywhere.

$$
V(x) = 0
$$

The time independent schrodinger's equation becomes

$$
-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m} \frac{d^2 \psi}{dx^2} = E \psi
$$

or, 

$$
\frac{d^2 \psi}{dx^2} = - k^2 \psi, \space k = \frac{\sqrt{2mE}}{\hbar}
$$

Therefore the general solution is,

$$
\psi(x) = A e^{ikx} + B e^{-ikx}
$$

Unlike, the infinite square well, there are no boundary conditions to restrict possible values of $k$ (and hence of $E$). The free particle can carry any positive energy. Adding the standard time dependence,

$$
\Psi(x,t) = A e^{ik(x-\frac{\hbar k}{2m}t)} + B e^{-ik(x+\frac{\hbar k}{2m}t)}
$$

Thus a free particle is a wave propagating waves.
