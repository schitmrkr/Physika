### The Uncertainty Principle

#### Proof of generalized uncertainty principle

For any observable $A$, we have

$$
\sigma_A^2 =\langle (\hat{A} - \langle A \rangle) \Psi | (\hat{A} - \langle A \rangle) \Psi \rangle = \langle f|f \rangle
$$

where, $f = (\hat{A} - \langle A \rangle) \Psi.$

Similarly for $B$, 

$$
\sigma_B^2 = \langle g|g \rangle
$$

where, $g = (\hat{B} - \langle B \rangle) \Psi.$

Therefore, with Schwarz Inequality,

$$
\sigma_A^2 \sigma_B^2 = \langle f|f \rangle \langle g|g \rangle \ge |\langle f|g \rangle|^2
$$

For complex number,

$$
|z|^2 = [Re(z)]^2 + [Im(z)]^2 \ge [Im(z)]^2 = [\frac{1}{2i}(z - z^*)]^2
$$

Therefore,

$$
\sigma_A^2 \sigma_B^2 \ge (\frac{1}{2i}[\langle f|g\rangle - \langle g|f \rangle])^2
$$

Exploiting the hermiticity

$$
\langle f|g \rangle = \langle (\hat{A} -A)\Psi | (\hat{B}-B)\Psi \rangle = \langle \Psi | (\hat{A} -A)(\hat{B}-B)\Psi \rangle
$$

$$
= \langle \Psi | (\hat{A}\hat{B}- \hat{A}B - A\hat{B} + AB) \Psi \rangle
$$

$$
= \langle \hat{A} \hat{B} \rangle - \langle A \rangle \langle B \rangle
$$

Similarly,

$$
\langle g|f \rangle = \langle \hat{B} \hat{A} \rangle - \langle A \rangle \langle B \rangle
$$

so,

$$
\langle f|g \rangle - \langle g|f \rangle = \langle \hat{A} \hat{B} \rangle - \langle \hat{B} \hat{A} \rangle = \langle [\hat{A}, \hat{B}]\rangle
$$

Conclusion:

$$
\sigma_A^2 \sigma_B^2 \ge (\frac{1}{2i} \langle [\hat{A}, \hat{B}] \rangle)^2
$$

This is the generalized uncertainty principle.

The first pair is position and momentum.

$$
[\hat{x}, \hat{p}] = -i \hbar
$$

So,

$$
\sigma_x^2 \sigma_p^2 \ge (\frac{1}{2i} (-i\hbar))^2 = (\frac{\hbar}{2})^2
$$

Therefore,

$$
\sigma_x \sigma_p \ge \frac{\hbar}{2}
$$
