### The spectrum of Hydrogen

$$
E_\lambda = E_i - E_f = -13.6 eV (\frac{1}{n_i^2} - \frac{1}{n_f^2})
$$

Also,

$$
E_\lambda = h \nu
$$

So,

$$
\frac{1}{\lambda} = R (\frac{1}{n_i^2} - \frac{1}{n_f^2})
$$

where,

$$
R = \frac{m_e}{4 \pi c \hbar^3} (\frac{e^2}{4 \pi \epsilon_0})^2 = 1.097 \times 10^7 m^{-1}
$$

is known as Rydberg constant.
